About

Technology with a purpose.

A short introduction to my academic background and interests.

Dr. Reina Setiawan is a faculty member of the Computer Science Department at Bina Nusantara University (BINUS University), Jakarta, Indonesia. Her academic work includes computer science research and the application of information technology in learning environments.

Her published research covers Indonesian-language stemming, topic discovery in online discussion forums, and the relationship between IT usage, IT adoption, innovation capabilities, and hybrid-learning performance.

This website is a starting point for sharing academic interests and selected publications. It will be updated as further profile details and research links are added.
